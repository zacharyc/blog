## Coffee

I find it hard to talk about good coffee every week, but since it's been a couple of weeks, I have some good notes.

### [Tamp](https://tampbristol.com)

Bristol, RI

[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/reviews/34b8c6e3-2831-440e-9566-d144ace244fd)

Someone in the business recommended this place, and I have to admit I'm impressed. They source their own beans from Italy, which made me concerned that it would be super dark coffee, but the Cortado I had was amazing. I also got a sweetened latte to test it and was impressed with that, too. It wasn't just all sugar; worth checking out.

### [NewberySt](https://newberyst.com)

Brookline, MA

[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/reviews/0a2ac330-8b5f-4a77-a070-f65140ed1f1e)

This place is a pop-up from my favorite roasters from last years [Hanukkah Coffee Box](https://www.hanukkahcoffeebox.com). I've been trying to make a pop-up and meet the team behind this roaster for years, and I have to say I wasn't disappointed. They are awesome. Their Salt bread was also great, but I got a Choco Berry "Funky" pour-over that did taste like both chocolate and berries.

### [TLC Coffee Roasters](https://www.tlccoffeeroasters.com)

West Kingston, RI

[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/entity/a4429b69-d42f-4ada-941c-bf13bab59f6f)

Sometimes I work in Hope Valley, and that means that I drive past this place like crazy. I've been there three times now, and it is a cute place, and I want to like it, but the coffee just isn't that good. Don't know why I keep coming back. I guess I keep hoping I will find the one thing that is good.

## Work

I've been doing a lot of different projects for work; here are some of them:

### [Authentic Auctions](https://www.authenticauctions.com)

Did a new redesign of the homepage here. That is a pretty tame way of saying I moved it off self-serving and onto a database-backed service. The long-term goal is to build a bunch of systems that work for the project and let us better serve our customers without requiring a redeploy every time we add a new car.

It still feels like an AI site, though, so there is definitely some design work to do there.

### [Rollin Renovations](https://www.rollinrenovations.com)

We are still doing a bunch of work in the construction space. We have built a couple of decks, built out a door jamb, and also started work on installing some windows and remodeling a bathroom. Learning a lot and having fun with many different projects.
