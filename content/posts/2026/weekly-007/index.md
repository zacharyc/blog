## Coffee

I find it hard to talk about good coffee every week, but since it's been a couple of weeks, I have some good notes.

### [Tamp](https://tampbristol.com)

Bristol, RI
[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/reviews/34b8c6e3-2831-440e-9566-d144ace244fd)
This place was recommended by someone in the business and I have to admit I'm impressed with it. They source their own beans from Italy, which made me concerned that it would be super dark coffee, but the cortado I had was amazing. I also got a sweetened latte to test it and was impressed with that, too. It wasn't just all sugar; worth checking out.

### [NewberySt](https://newberyst.com)

Brookline, MA
[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/reviews/0a2ac330-8b5f-4a77-a070-f65140ed1f1e)
This place is a popup from my favorite roasters from last years [Hanukkah Coffee Box](https://www.hanukkahcoffeebox.com). I've been trying to make a popup and meet the team behind this roaster for years, and I have to say I wasn't disappointed. They are awesome. Their Salt bread was also great, but I got a Choco Berry "Funky" pour over that did taste like both chocolate and berries.

### [TLC Coffee Roasters](https://www.tlccoffeeroasters.com)

West Kingston, RI
[Revi Link](https://revi-it.com/entity/a4429b69-d42f-4ada-941c-bf13bab59f6f)
Sometimes I work in Hope Valley and that means that I drive past this place like crazy. I've now been three times and it is a cute place and I want to like it, but the coffee just isn't that good. Don't know why I keep coming back. I guess I keep hoping I will find the one thing that is good.
